## Section 1: Execution Queue

No entries met the full verification bar today.

To qualify, a candidate needed **all** of: confirmed US HQ + street address, confirmed stage (seed–Series B), confirmed headcount under ~50, confirmed in-office (not remote-first) evidence, a founder X handle confirmed via an independent indexed source, **and** a specific, real, linkable recent post that isn't a funding announcement or hiring post.

Research this round (10 companies investigated in depth, listed in full in Section 3) consistently broke down at the last one or two links in that chain:

- **One candidate (Paper / Stephen Haney) cleared every bar except in-office** — real handle, real verified post, right stage and size — but Paper's own blog states the team is "fully remote, with team members across the Americas, Europe, and Australia." That's a direct, explicit disqualifier under the ICP ("not remote-first"), so it was cut despite being the single best-documented candidate found.
- **Several candidates had confirmed, real X handles** (Jeremy Nixon @JvNixon at Infinity.inc; Gal Tal-Hochberg @itsgalth at Beacon Security; Aryo Patel @AryoPatel and Tinah Hong @tinahhong at Andera; Minseok Bae @minseokbae235 at Cheiron) **but no qualifying post could be verified** — either the account had no visible posts, or the only findable/linkable post was the funding announcement itself (explicitly lowest-priority per the comment guidance), or older posts found were 12+ months stale. Per the sourcing rules, a handle without a verified specific post does not go in the queue.
- **One candidate (Puneet Puri, Gritt) had strong in-office evidence and a plausible handle (@puneet_x)** but the handle could not be independently confirmed against actual post content, and several unrelated accounts with similar names created real ambiguity. Excluded rather than guessed.
- **Several companies never yielded a founder X handle at all** (StrongestLayer, Linker Finance, KredosAi, AegisAI, 1872) despite confirmed funding/stage/HQ data.

Full detail, sourcing, and confidence levels for every one of these are in Section 3.

**A tooling note, for transparency:** WebFetch was unavailable for this entire session — every attempted fetch, including a control fetch of a random Wikipedia page, returned HTTP 403 from the local egress proxy (organization policy, not a site-specific block). All research above was done via WebSearch snippets only, with no ability to directly browse company career pages, press releases, or X profiles. This is a meaningfully worse research condition than a normal run and is the primary reason the chain kept breaking at the handle→post step rather than earlier. If WebFetch access is restored, a re-run of today's candidates (especially Infinity.inc, Andera, and Gritt, which cleared every other bar) would likely surface qualifying posts that a search snippet alone didn't reveal.
